# Stardust Resolve

An unofficial JoJo-inspired survival adventure focused on **Part 3: Stardust Crusaders**.
Awaken a Stand, develop your willpower, master its techniques and face DIO alone or
with friends. This is an early **alpha**, with combat balance and progression still
being refined.

## Seven playable Part 3 Stands

- **Star Platinum:** precise strikes, barrages, frontal guard, Star Finger, arrow
  interception, Stand leap and advanced local time stop.
- **Hierophant Green:** Emerald Splash, a restraining tentacle and an emerald barrier.
- **Silver Chariot:** rapid sword attacks, armor release and a thrown rapier.
- **Magician’s Red:** fire techniques, Red Bind and life detection.
- **Hermit Purple:** arm-mounted vines, utility techniques and Hamon conduction.
- **The Fool:** sand abilities and a protective dome for nearby allies.
- **The World:** powerful melee attacks, thrown knives and local time stop.

Each Stand has its own presentation and techniques. Abilities respect line of sight,
team rules and server PvP settings. Cooldowns and willpower unlocks are part of the
survival progression.

## A survival journey

Search new desert temple chests for a **Stardust Arrow**, which can awaken one of the
six Crusader Stands. Visiting a temple can also reveal a **Disc Keeper** nearby.
Train against distinct hostile enemies to earn mastery and permanent willpower
milestones, then use discs to store and recover Stands.

The keeper offers a voluntary nighttime challenge against **DIO and The World**.
The encounter includes telegraphed attacks, knife volleys and time-stop phases.
Contributing players receive individual, persistent victory rewards; the final hit
is not required. Defeating DIO unlocks access to The World through its core and disc.
If your inventory is full, your reward remains available to claim later.

## Installation

- **Minecraft Java Edition 26.3**
- **Java 25**
- **NeoForge 26.3.0.95** (tested loader version)
- **GeckoLib 5.5.7** for NeoForge/Minecraft 26.3 — required dependency
- Install this mod and GeckoLib on **both client and server**.

The first alpha is **0.14.1-alpha.1**, network protocol **13**. Keep player and server
versions in sync. Existing 0.14.0 progression is preserved; make a world backup
before updating an experimental installation.

## Getting started

Use **/stand journey** for your next objective, **/stand progress** for your abilities
and mastery, and **/stand claim** for pending rewards.

| Default key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| R | Summon or dismiss your Stand |
| Z / X / C | Primary / secondary / utility technique |
| V | Advanced technique; Star Finger for Star Platinum |
| B / J | Star Platinum interception / leap |
| G | Local time stop for compatible users |

Keys can be rebound in Controls. Advanced techniques require the corresponding
willpower and may be unavailable while another action is active.

## Alpha limitations

DIO fights reset when their chunks unload or the server closes. Your keeper,
mastery, willpower, completed victories and reward claims remain saved. Balance,
audio levels and compatibility with large modpacks or external shaders need more
player feedback. Echoes ACT 1 and Little Feet are retained legacy prototypes;
Whitesnake and other JoJo parts are future expansions, outside this alpha's scope.
