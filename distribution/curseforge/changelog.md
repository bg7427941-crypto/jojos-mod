# 0.14.1-alpha.1 — Stardust Crusaders alpha

- Seven playable Part 3 Stands with animated models and distinct techniques.
- Persistent willpower, mastery and Stand disc storage/recovery.
- Natural desert temple keepers, journey guidance and individual milestones.
- DIO encounter with The World, announced attacks, knife volleys and local time stop.
- Cooperative participation rewards and pending claims for full inventories.
- Advanced abilities including Hierophant's tentacle/barrier, thrown rapier,
  life detection, Hamon conduction, sand dome and physical knives.
- Individual movement budgets inside another user's stopped time.
- Updated inventory sprites, English/Spanish text and subtitles.
- Two audio files variants: anime-alpha preserves the 25 collected recordings;
  public-alpha uses original synthesized effects.

Requires Minecraft 26.3, Java 25, NeoForge 26.3.0.95 and GeckoLib 5.5.7.
Install on client and server. Network protocol: 13.

Validation includes 168 native GameTests and 14 artifact checks. Human feedback
on balance, progression and sound mixing is still needed. Active DIO fights reset
on chunk unload/server shutdown; completed progression and rewards remain saved.
